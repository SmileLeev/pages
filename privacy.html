Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-06

Ring Number Defense is an offline single-player puzzle game.

Privacy contact email: lijianhy1990@gmail.com

Information We Collect

The first release of Ring Number Defense does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal information.

The app stores gameplay progress locally on your device, including best wave, best score, and selected theme. This information is used only to provide game features inside the app.

Advertising And Purchases

The first release does not include advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Analytics And Crash Reports

The first release is intended to work without a custom analytics backend. Google Play may provide aggregated install, device, and crash information to the developer through Google Play Console. This information is handled according to Google's policies and is used to improve app stability.

If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or purchases are added in a future release, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Children

Ring Number Defense is a general-audience puzzle game. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

The app does not sell personal information. The first release does not share gameplay progress with third-party services.

Data Retention And Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. You can delete this local data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage through your device settings.
